Gentle Steps — Offline Self-Care Mini App
🌿 Overview
Gentle Steps is a lightweight, private self-care web app designed to support your mental well-being. It helps you track your mood, reflect through journaling, and practice calming exercises — all in a simple, distraction-free environment.
________________________________________
✨ Features
•	Mood tracking
•	Simple journaling
•	Breathing exercise for relaxation
•	Works offline after first load
•	Download/export your personal data
•	No account required (100% private)
________________________________________
🔒 Privacy
Your data is stored locally on your device using your browser.
Nothing is uploaded or shared — your information stays completely private.
________________________________________
📱 How to Use
1.	Open the gentle-steps.html file in your browser
2.	Use the app normally (internet needed only the first time)
3.	After loading once, the app can be used offline
4.	(Optional) Add to your home screen for quick access

💾 Exporting Your Data
You can download your journal entries anytime using the export feature inside the app.

⚙️ Requirements
•	A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.)
•	No installation or login required

📌 Notes
•	For best offline experience, open the app once while connected to the internet
•	Works best on both desktop and mobile devices

💡 Purpose
Gentle Steps is designed to be a simple, safe space to pause, reflect, and take small steps toward better mental wellness.
